# Design Agent Prompt — Bestehen / Corpus
## Create the Complete Operations UI, UX, and Design System

You are the lead product designer and design-systems designer responsible for turning the fully shaped **Bestehen / Corpus** product into a complete, coherent, implementation-ready design for its owner-operated internal experience.

Your task is to create the **actual UI/UX design artifact and reusable design system** for the complete Corpus operations, review, governance, health, and change-impact experience. Do not stop at a mood board, a dashboard concept, or a few showcase tables. Work systematically until every required operational state has a designed home, every consequential action has a safe reusable pattern, and a later architecture/engineering agent can implement the product without inventing major workflow behavior.

This is a **design task**, not new product shaping, not architecture selection, and not production-code implementation. Preserve settled product semantics. You may make visual, information-hierarchy, interaction, layout, responsive, component, and workflow-presentation decisions where the source documents intentionally leave design open. You must not collapse provenance, authenticity/confidence, rights, difficulty/relevance, QA readiness, or content identity/independence into one status; weaken audit or takedown behavior; create learner-facing features; or turn Corpus into an enterprise administration suite.

## 2. Product Context You Must Preserve

Bestehen helps a learner move from roughly A1 German toward **passing TELC B1 by learning through exam preparation**. The umbrella has two connected products:

- **Bestehen / Corpus** gathers broad A2/B1 exam evidence, preserves provenance, separates confidence from usage rights, structures and deduplicates material, validates it, and produces trustworthy exam intelligence.
- **Bestehen / App** is the learner-facing exam coach. It owns learner state, original practice, feedback, coaching, and Readiness.

Corpus's primary customer is **Bestehen / App**. Its primary human user at launch is the **single project owner/operator**. Corpus is not a learner-facing exercise library, public corpus browser, source mirror, generic scraper, public API product, multi-tenant SaaS, or enterprise moderation platform.

The designed experience should help the owner answer:

1. **What needs attention now, and why?**
2. **What evidence and state justify this decision?**
3. **Which capability is blocked, limited, eligible, or stale?**
4. **What is the smallest safe action?**
5. **What changed downstream after that action?**
6. **Is Corpus trustworthy, maintainable, and within budget?**

The UI should feel like calm scientific operations tooling: compact, evidence-rich, exact, and safe. It should make uncertainty inspectable without making every row alarming. It should favor tables, detail panels, comparisons, state timelines, and concise health summaries over decorative dashboards.

## 4. Non-Negotiable Product and Design Guardrails

### 4.1 Keep the six truth axes separate

Never replace the following with one generic badge, health score, or `valid` state:

1. source/provenance;
2. authenticity/evidence confidence;
3. rights/usage;
4. difficulty and Generic-B1/TELC relevance;
5. extraction/QA readiness by capability;
6. content identity, derivative lineage, and analytical independence.

A record can be official but `REFERENCE_ONLY`; reusable but low-confidence; correctly parsed but quarantined; high-TELC-relevance but weakly supported; or duplicate for one metric while distinct for another. The interface must make these combinations understandable without forcing the operator to remember hidden coupling.

### 4.2 Preserve uncertainty and locality

Design explicit treatment for `unknown`, `not_applicable`, `disputed`, `review_required`, `unresolved_dependency`, `incomplete`, `provisional`, and locally blocked states. Missing data must not look like zero, false, passed, or failed.

High-risk decisions use the smallest relevant scope. A trusted parent does not visually rescue a bad child. One bad Item or Asset must not make an otherwise useful Exam look universally failed when unaffected capabilities remain eligible.

### 4.3 Publication is capability-scoped

Do not design a universal `production ready` switch. Show capability-specific eligibility such as structure analytics, correctness/scoring, taxonomy, pattern intelligence, exact source reuse, and independent prevalence. A unit may be normal-eligible for one use, limited/provisional for another, and held for a third.

### 4.4 Preserve provenance, rights, and source expression

Manual changes are curated corrections, overrides, relationship decisions, or status decisions. They do not silently rewrite the observed source. Show observed value, active interpretation, derivation/decision origin, rationale, and history where relevant.

Unknown rights never imply permission. Allowing a source does not grant authenticity or rights. Takedown/security blocks are stronger than ordinary acquisition blocks and must not be reversed by casual controls.

### 4.5 Preserve duplicate neutrality

All Source Observations remain visible. Duplicate decisions distinguish relationship class, canonical provenance anchor, preferred processing representation, and metric-specific analytical independence. Never imply that confirming a duplicate deletes provenance or that many mirrors increase prevalence.

### 4.6 Preserve the App boundary

Corpus may expose pattern blueprints, intelligence, eligibility, provenance summaries, versioning, and downstream impact. Do not create learner accounts, learner attempts, Readiness, daily coaching, practice delivery, feedback, or learner progress screens.

The preferred relationship is:

**eligible Corpus evidence → abstract pattern/constraint blueprint → original App exercise**.

Direct source reuse is exceptional and rights-governed.

### 4.7 Preserve one-owner scope and cost discipline

Do not introduce roles, assignments, approvals, comments, SLAs, billing, public submission, customer support, collaboration feeds, or enterprise BI as launch requirements. A polished design may still be simple: one prioritized inbox, reusable detail surfaces, explicit actions, and an actionable health vector.

Avoid designs that imply always-on AI, live model calls for every view, expensive full-document re-analysis on navigation, or storage of many redundant previews. The UI may surface asynchronous/batched state and cached results.

### 4.8 Preserve security boundaries

External input is untrusted data. Source HTML, documents, archives, media, and text must never appear to have active authority. Design sanitized previews, explicit unsafe/unsupported/blocked states, bounded archive information, external-link warnings where useful, and no rendering that implies macros/scripts/embedded content execute.

Source text that resembles instructions to an AI remains source evidence, not a command. Secrets, authenticated acquisition, and operator authority must not appear in source content or logs.

### 4.9 Preserve the brand without making an internal tool theatrical

Use the Bestehen identity—competent, calm, evidence-driven, practical, restrained—but tune it for operations. Do not add mascots, gamification, celebratory confetti, fake certainty, TELC endorsement, or marketing-heavy empty states. `Bestehen / Corpus` is a sibling product under the umbrella identity, not a separate consumer brand campaign.

## 5. Required Information Architecture

Use the smallest coherent navigation that gives every launch requirement a clear owner. Recommended primary areas:

1. **Inbox** — prioritized exceptions and saved/specialized queue views.
2. **Sources** — discovery, manual intake, source registry, allow/block, origin/mirror/dead-source context.
3. **Corpus** — search/browse canonical records and open the reusable Record/Review Detail.

Benchmark, audit, taxonomy, and App-impact views may be subareas, tabs, saved views, or contextual routes rather than top-level destinations. Do not create eight unrelated products. One Inbox plus specialized views is the settled default.

Include global search only if it searches stable identities and useful fields safely; it must not become an excuse to omit navigable queues and context.

## 10. Required Prototype Flows

Create connected, reviewable prototypes for at least these flows, including important recovery branches:

### Flow A — Discover to capability-scoped publication

Candidate discovered → triage → accept for ingestion → security preflight → processing with one partial scope → review → capability eligibility → publish eligible intelligence → App-impact summary.

### Flow B — Extraction failure and scoped reprocessing

Inbox priority → failure detail → inspect prior methods and blocked capability → choose changed method/earliest stage → cost/impact confirmation → background reprocess → partial success/conflict branch → resulting eligibility.

### Flow C — Authenticity uncertainty

High-TELC-relevance low-confidence record → inspect local and corroborating evidence → accept limited influence or mark disputed → rationale → audit → downstream analytics state changes without changing rights.

### Flow D — Rights takedown

Takedown/high-risk rights item → scope and mirrors → immediate affected-use hold → governed decision → source-expression suppression/removal → prevention block → recomputation impact → completion audit. Never make this a casual generic delete flow.

### Flow E — Duplicate family decision

Ambiguous pair/cluster → inspect similarity and meaningful differences → decide relationship → separately choose provenance anchor/processing representation/independence → preview analytics impact → confirm → recompute.

### Flow F — Taxonomy correction

Candidate assignment → inspect task/answer/reasoning evidence → remap/broaden or keep provisional → version/impact summary → confirm → downstream retag/recompute state.

### Flow G — Benchmark regression

New method/model candidate → capability/slice regression → failed holdout or incident replay → block promotion → inspect diff → revise/re-run → qualified promotion with immutable benchmark reference.

### Flow H — Corpus health triage

Health warning → identify hard blocker rather than aggregate score → open affected queue slice → take smallest safe action → return to updated health and cost state.

## 11. Content and UX-Writing Requirements

Use realistic, concise operations copy. Do not use lorem ipsum, fake data-science jargon, or vague buttons such as `Fix`, `Resolve`, or `Make valid` when the action has governed meaning.

- factual, neutral, and evidence-specific;
- explicit about scope and uncertainty;

- precise about affected capabilities and downstream consequences;

Use plausible German exam/provider/task labels and source evidence. Preserve German characters. Do not reproduce substantial protected exam content; short original or safely abstracted examples are sufficient. Do not imply official TELC affiliation.

- no drag-only taxonomy/relationship editing;
- destructive confirmations that explain exact scope and retain keyboard focus;
- long URL/hash/identifier copying without forcing screen-reader users through noisy repeated text.

Dense does not mean inaccessible. Use progressive disclosure instead of tiny text or color-only shorthand.

## 13. Loading, Empty, Failure, Conflict, and Recovery States

For each major flow, design:

- initial loading and skeletons;
- empty Corpus and empty queue;
- delayed or stale cached results;
- background job pending and completed after navigation;
- partial processing success;
- transient retry versus exhausted retry requiring review;
- budget-deferred and paid-job approval gate;
- unsafe file/security hold;
- unsupported format or encrypted/password-protected input;
- lost network/session expiry while a rationale draft exists;
- conflicting operator change or late automated result;
- curator override retained after rerun;
- source removed, unavailable, or changed;
- rights/takedown hold and downstream recomputation;
- benchmark not instantiated, not run, failed, or superseded;
- no decision-grade analytical result;
- Corpus → App contract version incompatibility;
- action succeeds only for part of the selected scope.

Never show an indefinite AI spinner. Show the durable state, what is safe to do next, and whether leaving the page is harmless.

## 14. Design-System Documentation and Engineering Handoff

Document enough that implementation does not reverse-engineer the artifact:

- token/variable names and values;
- component names, anatomy, variants, states, and composition rules;
- responsive transformations and safe desktop-only tasks;
- table/filter/search behavior and preserved queue context;
- interaction, keyboard, focus, validation, loading, error, conflict, and recovery behavior;
- density rules and German/source-content constraints;
- security/sanitized-preview annotations;
- exact visual mapping for the six truth axes and all uncertainty states;
- capability-eligibility and audit/change-impact state models;
- destructive/takedown action safeguards;
- chart/visualization semantics and accessible alternatives;
- launch-required versus deferred labels;
- sample realistic copy;
- source-document traceability;
- every normal design assumption that later architecture may rely on.

Specify the UI state/data requirement, not backend endpoints, physical schemas, queue products, cloud vendors, model providers, or implementation frameworks.

Create a Markdown handoff document suitable for committing alongside the design artifacts, recommended as `Design/DESIGN-HANDOFF.md`, containing source hierarchy, artifact index, design decisions, component/state contracts, responsive/accessibility rules, flow coverage, launch/deferred boundary, and open assumptions.

## 15. Coverage and Quality-Control Checklist

Create a coverage matrix mapping every major Final Corpus Product Specification area to:

- screen(s);
- component/pattern(s);
- responsive coverage;
- accessibility coverage;
- prototype flow where applicable;
- status: complete, represented by a reusable variant, or explicitly deferred by the specification.

Run these checks before declaring the design complete.

### Product truth

- [ ] The six truth axes remain separately visible and cannot be mistaken for one score.
- [ ] Unknown/N/A/disputed/incomplete/provisional states are explicit.
- [ ] Local child defects do not falsely invalidate unaffected parent capabilities.
- [ ] Publication is capability-scoped.
- [ ] Rights never upgrade confidence and allow controls grant neither rights nor authenticity.
- [ ] Duplicates preserve observations and do not manufacture prevalence.
- [ ] Manual corrections preserve observed source truth and audit history.
- [ ] Takedown/security actions are stronger than ordinary rejection/blocking.
- [ ] Corpus → App remains pattern-first and Corpus stores no learner data.
- [ ] No public Corpus, learner, collaboration, or enterprise features were introduced.

### Experience completeness

- [ ] Entry/access, Inbox, Sources, Corpus detail, Runs, and Health are designed.
- [ ] All eight specialized queues are represented.
- [ ] Discovery/manual intake, allow/block, partial processing, failure, review, reprocessing, publication, audit, and downstream impact are covered.
- [ ] Authenticity, rights, duplicate, taxonomy, QA, benchmark, analytics, and App-contract states are covered.
- [ ] Every required flow includes important failure/recovery branches.
- [ ] Desktop and compact-laptop designs are complete; tablet transformations are documented.
- [ ] Destructive and high-impact decisions show exact scope and consequences.

### Design-system quality

- [ ] Foundations use variables/tokens and meet contrast requirements.
- [ ] Components are reusable rather than forked per queue.
- [ ] Tables, filters, detail panels, comparisons, forms, timelines, and health views have complete states.
- [ ] Responsive rules are explicit.
- [ ] Realistic content replaces lorem ipsum.
- [ ] WCAG 2.2 AA behavior and visual alternatives are annotated.
- [ ] No status relies only on color.

### Identity, restraint, and cost

- [ ] The UI feels calm, credible, operational, and part of Bestehen.
- [ ] Dense evidence remains understandable through hierarchy and disclosure.
- [ ] No mascot, gamification, fake certainty, or TELC endorsement appears.
- [ ] No scalar health score hides hard blockers.
- [ ] The design does not imply always-on AI or enterprise operations.
- [ ] Deferred conveniences remain labeled rather than designed as launch scope.

## 16. Working Method

1. Read the three source documents completely.
2. Summarize source authority, product invariants, launch scope, and deferred boundaries inside the artifact.
3. Build the information architecture, screen inventory, six-axis state matrix, action-risk matrix, and prototype map before polishing screens.
4. Establish variables/tokens and shared table/detail/review/action components.
5. Design Flow A end to end at desktop fidelity to validate the system.
6. Extend the reusable system across all queues, review types, failures, destructive actions, and health states.
7. Validate laptop/tablet transformations and keyboard/accessibility behavior.
8. Connect Flows A–H and their recovery branches.
9. Run product-truth, rights/security, accessibility, responsiveness, cost/scope, and coverage reviews.
10. Finish `DESIGN-HANDOFF.md` and explicitly list assumptions and source-driven deferrals.

Do not ask the owner to choose between ordinary visual or layout options. Use professional judgment and the provided variables. Surface only a question that genuinely blocks a correct design, and include a recommended default.

## 17. Final Deliverables

Deliver all of the following:

1. A complete organized high-fidelity Corpus operations design artifact.
2. A reusable variable/token-based design system and component library.
3. Desktop and compact-laptop screens for the entire required inventory.
4. Tablet/responsive behavior documentation and proofs for safe supported tasks.
5. Connected prototypes for Flows A–H with major recovery branches.
6. Realistic operations copy and representative safe source/exam data.
7. Accessibility annotations and a WCAG 2.2 AA review.
8. Screen/component/state coverage matrix against the Final Corpus Product Specification.
9. `Design/DESIGN-HANDOFF.md` or an equivalent Markdown handoff artifact.
10. A concise final summary stating what was designed, what is covered, which variable defaults were enhanced, which source-driven features remain deferred, which assumptions were made, and whether any genuine blocker remains.

The task is complete only when the result represents the **whole maintainable Bestehen / Corpus operations experience**, not merely an Inbox or health dashboard. A later architect or engineer should be able to identify the correct screen, state, hierarchy, safe action, uncertainty treatment, accessibility behavior, and responsive rule for every launch-critical operational moment without inventing a second product.
