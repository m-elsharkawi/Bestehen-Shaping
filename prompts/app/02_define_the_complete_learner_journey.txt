    ## Project context you must preserve

Bestehen is a personal project whose purpose is to help a learner move from roughly A1 German toward **passing TELC B1**, using exam preparation as the learning method rather than a conventional language-course curriculum.

The umbrella product has two separate but connected products:

- **Bestehen / Corpus** — the exam-data and intelligence product. It gathers a broad A2/B1 German exam corpus from official and unofficial sources, preserves provenance, evaluates confidence/authenticity separately from usage rights, extracts structured exam/skill information, deduplicates material, and produces intelligence that can guide the learning product.
- **Bestehen / App** — the learner-facing **exam coach**. It teaches German through exam-shaped practice, diagnoses mistakes, repairs weaknesses, adapts daily training, evaluates all relevant exam skills, and centers progress on defensible TELC B1 exam readiness rather than generic lesson completion.

Important product principles already agreed:

1. **Learn German through TELC**, rather than finish a traditional A1→A2→B1 course first.
2. The first usable product should eventually feel **complete across the whole experience**. Implementation may be phased, but product shaping should cover the complete product from the start.
3. The target exam is TELC B1, but **Corpus should be broader than TELC** and may use A2/B1 material from TELC, Goethe, ÖSD, DTZ, educational sources, unofficial collections, public repositories, archives, teacher material, and other sources that appear useful.
4. Unofficial material is acceptable as corpus evidence when it looks credible enough, but **authenticity/confidence and usage rights must be modeled separately**.
5. Real/reference exams should help define what is tested. The App should be able to practice those patterns with original content where direct reuse is not appropriate.
6. The learner-facing identity is an **exam coach**: diagnostic, practical, strategic, calm, evidence-driven, and focused on passing.
7. **Readiness** is the central progress concept. It must eventually be evidence-based and must respect the independent written/oral pass constraints of the target exam.
8. This is a **personal project, at least initially**. Monetary cost matters. Prefer designs that are cheap to operate, easy to maintain, and can scale gradually. Do not assume an enterprise budget, always-on expensive AI calls, or costly infrastructure unless the benefit clearly justifies it.
